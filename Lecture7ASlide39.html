Richtmyer-Meshkov Instability

The Richtmyer-Meshkov instability occurs when a shock wave impulsively accelerates a perturbed, corrugated interface between two fluids of different densities, during which vorticity is deposited through the baroclinic torque term $(\nabla \rho \times \nabla p)/\rho^{2}$, leading to the growth of interface perturbations.
